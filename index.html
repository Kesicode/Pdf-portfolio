Introduction

Building intelligent electronics that solve real-world engineering challenges.

I am a third-year Electronics and Communication Engineering student with a focused interest in embedded systems, custom PCB hardware, robotics, and IoT. My work spans the full hardware stack from schematic design and PCB layout to firmware development and system integration.

Why I Build

I enjoy building technology that bridges hardware, firmware, and software to solve practical engineering problems. Every project is an opportunity to learn, iterate, and create solutions that are reliable, scalable, and meaningful.

Engineering Focus

Most of my work centers on designing custom development boards and embedded solutions using STM32, ESP32, and RP2040 microcontrollers. I design boards in KiCad from single-layer breakouts to multi-layer development platforms and write the firmware that runs on them.

Beyond Hardware

I also enjoy UI/UX design, web development, and product thinking. I believe strong engineering combines technical precision with thoughtful user experience.

Career Objective

To become a skilled embedded systems and hardware engineer capable of designing intelligent electronics products, reliable embedded platforms, and innovative automation systems while continuously learning emerging technologies.
